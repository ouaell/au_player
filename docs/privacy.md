# Privacy policy (MS-052, draft for GitHub Pages + Play listing)

AU Player plays your local files and streams you open. It has no accounts,
no analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no built-in content.

- Media access: videos/images you open (MediaStore/SAF grants you approve).
- Network: streams and pages you open; reader image downloads use the
  cookies/headers of pages you visited. No data leaves the device except to
  the servers you chose to open.
- Crash reports: local log only, shared only when you tap Share.
- Ads (if ever enabled): isolated provider, consent via Google UMP in
  EEA/UK, never during playback/reading. Currently: no ads ship.

Contact: repository issue tracker (see About screen when published).
